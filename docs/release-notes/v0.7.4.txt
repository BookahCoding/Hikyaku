Hikyaku 0.7.4 - Irises theme

- Adds Irises, a ninth Hokusai-inspired theme: iris violet on lavender paper in Light and pale violet on aubergine in Dark.
- Choose Irises in Settings > Appearance. Both variants have tested text contrast and are distinct from all eight earlier themes.

Update from Settings > Updates > Check for updates. Your settings and sign-in are kept.
For a first installation, use Hikyaku-Setup-0.7.4.exe.
